Our claims

NO SECURITY
THEATER.

Most vendors here will tell you their protection is absolute. It never is. Here is exactly where our limits are, so you can decide with your eyes open.

Client-side isn't secrecy

Code running on a machine someone else owns can, given enough effort, be reverse-engineered. open and local raise extraction cost — they don't make it unachievable, and we won't pretend otherwise.

Real secrecy lives server-side

If a computation must stay secret, it belongs in split or enclave. That's the honest boundary, and the one we build toward.

Offline weakens revocation

Air-gapped deployments run on time-boxed leases, so revocation lands at lease expiry or reconnect rather than instantly. You choose the trade; we document it.
